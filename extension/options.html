Setup

Connect your bookmark shelf.

Point the extension at your Nextcloud, use a revocable app password, and keep your bookmarks in the folders you already use.

01 · Connection

Nextcloud account

→

Use a revocable Nextcloud app password (Settings → Security → App passwords), especially with two-factor authentication. It is stored locally in Firefox.

02 · Folders

Your folders

+

Create and rename folders here. Deleting folders stays in Nextcloud.

03 · Import

Bring Firefox bookmarks over

⇢

Manual one-way import into Nextcloud. Nothing is changed or deleted in Firefox.

Allow Firefox bookmarks access to choose what to import.

Existing URLs are skipped. With folder recreation on, the Firefox folder tree is rebuilt in Nextcloud; otherwise everything goes into the selected folder.
